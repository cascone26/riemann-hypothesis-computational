Third and widest pass on RH. Same reach-by-the-move rule as round II, pushed into fields nobody checks for a number theory problem: ecology, immunology, cryptography, linguistics, law, music, meteorology, and the exact procedural history of how Fermat's Last Theorem actually got solved.

Zero overlap with round I (sphere packing, function fields, PT-symmetry, Beurling, RMT local law, Connes–Consani, Lee–Yang, automated proving, ML attribution, tensor networks, DPPs) or round II (spin glass, RG, quasicrystals, Hopfield/EBM, game theory, TDA, complex networks, multifractal, RIP, p-bits, dynamical systems, category theory, assembly theory). Checked against LEDGER's DEAD ENDS — nothing reopened.
Marquee find: May's 1972 random-matrix stability criterion for ecosystems (σ√(SC)<1) — real food webs survive it by not being random: weak interactions dominate, structure is modular. Direct, unused structural question for τ_N: does positivity survive because the prime-comb coupling matrix is food-web-structured, not despite looking random?
Frame · 5 wilder inversions

Reframings this round

  1. 01

    Predictability horizon

    Weather forecasting has a rigorous Lyapunov-time beyond which only statistics are predictable, not individual states. Maybe the right target isn't "prove RH" but a predictability-horizon theorem, with the on-line property falling out as a consequence of the statistical regime.

  2. 02

    The Wiles template

    FLT was solved by finding an unrelated "shadow conjecture" (Taniyama–Shimura) and inventing new machinery to prove that instead. Nobody has systematically searched for RH's shadow conjecture.

  3. 03

    Meta-spin-glass on the project itself

    Apply round II's ruggedness diagnostic to the research process, not the math: is the sequence of dead-ends in LEDGER rugged (thrashing) or smooth (converging)?

  4. 04

    Hostile referee, not hostile adversary

    If a complete proof landed tomorrow, what would a skeptical referee attack first? Build that case-based skepticism in now, using the precedent of every historical false RH-proof submission.

  5. 05

    A different sense channel

    What would positivity holding-with-room-to-spare sound like versus barely holding? Sonify τ_N's spectrum and listen for dissonance — a channel none of the scalar diagnostics use.
