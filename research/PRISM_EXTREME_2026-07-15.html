Frame · 6 inversions

Tony-flavored reframings

  1. 01

    Coastline Principle

    RH isn't fundamentally number theory — it's one instance of "positivity of a structured landscape against a discrete adversarial comb," recurring at the atomic scale (spin glasses), crystallographic scale (quasicrystals), neural scale (associative memory). Reach the right lens by affordance, not by field-of-origin.

  2. 02

    Moiré / dimensional-upgrade

    "Superpose-and-slide > move-and-remember; seam dominates." Stop treating N→∞ as the only lever. Superpose two already-computed finite objects (two lsq values, two N's, real-prime τ vs. bad-Beurling τ) and read the seam directly.

  3. 03

    Reach-by-the-move

    Tony's own documented fix — keying the Codex by keyword failed (`project_reach_experiment_operation_tag`). Key by Forge-thread operation instead (verify-before-commit, anchor-to-exemplar, deposit) and let that surface the personas below.

  4. 04

    Nexus five missing organs, on the project itself

    Decide / continuity / world-model / uncertainty / verify. Continuity and verify are strong (PROCESS.md, LEDGER). Decide, world-model, and uncertainty are the weak organs — no stated picture of what a proof looks like, no calibrated credence, no explicit next-experiment rule.

  5. 05

    Pre-mortem, Tony edition

    If the answer is already in the Codex, mis-filed. Don't grep for "Riemann" or "zeta" — grep for the operations the current attempts perform.

  6. 06

    Strip further via assembly theory

    Is there a well-defined "assembly index" for Spec(Z) built multiplicatively from primes — the way assembly theory measures a molecule's minimum construction-path length? A different pure-problem stripping than round 1's quadratic form.
