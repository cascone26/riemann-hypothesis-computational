That is a direct, previously-unused structural question for τ_N: **does positivity survive because the prime-comb coupling matrix is "food-web-structured" (weak-interaction-dominant, modular) rather than because of any generic argument?** Nobody has run that diagnostic on the existing operator.

## Frame — 5 wilder inversions

1. **Predictability horizon** (meteorology) — weather forecasting has a rigorous Lyapunov-time beyond which no method, present or future, predicts individual states, only statistics. Reframe: maybe RH isn't "provable" in the sense of pinning every zero — maybe the right target is a **predictability-horizon theorem**, proving a sharp boundary beyond which only statistical (GUE-type) behavior is accessible, with the on-line property holding as the statistical regime's own consequence.
2. **The Wiles template** (history of ideas) — Fermat's Last Theorem wasn't solved by attacking FLT. It was solved by first finding an unrelated-looking "shadow conjecture" (Taniyama–Shimura/modularity) whose truth implied FLT, then inventing wholly new machinery (Galois deformation theory, R=T) to prove *that*. Nobody has systematically searched for RH's shadow conjecture — a statement from a different field whose truth would imply RH via a reduction not yet attempted.
3. **Meta-spin-glass on the project itself** — apply round 2's ruggedness diagnostic not to τ_N but to the **research process**: is the sequence of dead-ends/live-angles in LEDGER exhibiting a rugged (many local optima, thrashing) or smooth (single basin, converging) landscape? A structural read on whether the project itself is stuck in a frustrated state.
4. **Hostile referee, not hostile adversary** (legal reasoning) — round 1's pre-mortem asked "why still open in 2036." This asks instead: if a complete proof were submitted tomorrow, what's the FIRST thing a skeptical referee would attack, using precedent-based reasoning (case-based reasoning against every historical false RH-proof submission)? Build that skepticism in now, not after a draft exists.
5. **Strip to a different sense channel** (acoustics/music) — what would positivity holding-with-room-to-spare *sound* like versus barely holding? Sonify τ_N's spectrum directly and listen for the equivalent of dissonance — a channel none of the existing scalar diagnostics use.

## Diverge (b) — the seven Codex lenses, run against RH directly

Round 2 used threads; this round runs the **lenses themselves** as seven distinct interrogation angles — a structurally different generator than either prior round's technique deck.

| Lens | Applied to RH |
|---|---|

| **Govern** | A written "constitution" file alongside OBSTRUCTION_MAP/LEDGER: explicit invariants that must hold for any future claim (never claim proof without independent second-method verification; never silently revise a closed obstruction) — makes round 2's no-silent-fork audit a standing rule instead of a one-time pass. |
